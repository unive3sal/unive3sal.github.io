## about

a little about me02

I’m Yuhao Deng (unive3sal). I work on AI engineering and architecture, and have worked at Ericsson, Alibaba Cloud, and Super Intelligence Inc. I started out implementing and optimizing algorithms at Ericsson. That got me interested in AI inference and parallel computing, and then in how to design the systems around them.

At Super Intelligence Inc., I designed the architecture for megahit, from cloud deployment to backend service boundaries. Its worker runs long-horizon agent tasks, with durable execution and recovery from interruptions. I also worked with researchers to put their memory research into the product. These days, I’m interested in AI agents, memory, and how they change application architecture.

When choosing an architecture, I start with what the company needs most and the metric it cares about. That guides the trade-offs between performance, delivery speed, and reliability. I also consider how much harder a design might make it for engineers and domain experts to work together.

I mostly write Rust, Python, and C++. I also contribute to cc-switch-cli, an open-source tool for AI coding assistants, where I’ve worked on the daemon, protocol compatibility, and CI. I’m looking for my next role, and I’d be glad to hear from people looking for collaborators or co-maintainers for open-source projects.

megahit/

AI growth platform · Agent architecture & evaluationJul–Nov 2026 · AI Engineer / Architect · Super Intelligence Inc.
architecture+

Megahit turns business goals into research, campaign plans, and execution workflows. I owned its backend architecture and migration to PydanticAI, with two priorities: keeping long-running work recoverable and making agent behavior testable.

01 · Long-horizon tasks & memory

“Post three times a week on X for us.” Prepare a weekly batch, wait for edits and approval, publish at the chosen local times, then continue into the next cycle.

I separated execution from browser streaming, with checkpoints, worker leases, and a durable activity feed to support recovery across disconnects and restarts. Content-bound approvals and an idempotent operation ledger kept publishing tied to the content the user approved.

Working with the memory-system researcher, I integrated shared memory into the runtime, tracking context sources and routing proposed updates through the memory service. Chat and campaign workflows could reuse relevant context while keeping execution state and access scopes separate.

02 · Evaluation datasets & automation

I built a versioned routing regression suite and extended it with incident-seeded multi-turn cases for forms, memory corrections, revisions, and concurrent requests. Deterministic checks ran in CI; live-model sweeps tracked behavioral changes. Missing configuration, incomplete coverage, and errored runs could not produce a passing verdict.

773 queries in a recorded full sweep · 25 Sep 2026

A historical evaluation snapshot with zero harness errors; results were tied to a specific build and model.

One regression test caught approvals expiring before the last post in a future weekly batch. I aligned approval lifetime with the preparation lead time and posting horizon.
